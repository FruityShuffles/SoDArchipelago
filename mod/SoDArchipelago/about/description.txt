[h1]Archipelago Randomizer[/h1]
Connects Shape of Dreams to an Archipelago multiworld. Achievements and world clears (worlds 1-5, per Traveler and difficulty) become location checks, and the Traveler, Memory, Essence and Lucid Dream unlocks, plus mastery and Stardust, are shuffled into the multiworld.

Each seed is played on its own profile; your normal save is never touched.

To generate a seed you need the Shape of Dreams apworld: download [b]shape_of_dreams.apworld[/b] from the [url=https://github.com/FruityShuffles/SoDArchipelago/releases]GitHub releases[/url], using the release that matches this mod's version. Setup guide: [url=https://github.com/FruityShuffles/SoDArchipelago/blob/main/apworld/shape_of_dreams/docs/setup_en.md]setup_en.md[/url].
